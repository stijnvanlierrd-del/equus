What happens next

No unexplained waiting list. You know in advance what you get, what it costs and when it can happen.

  1. 01

    We read your question

    Your enquiry is read personally. If more information is needed, we first ask a few specific questions.

  2. 02

    Proposal and quote

    You receive a proposal for the approach that fits best, with a clear price including travel costs.

  3. 03

    Scheduling the appointment

    Once agreed, we set a date. For a consultation you receive a short questionnaire beforehand, so the time on site is used well.
